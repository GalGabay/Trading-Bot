# Research holder election rule — choice still pending

This is a historical-simulation choice. It does not place orders, contact a
broker, spend money or change paper-risk settings. The previously approved

## Proposed rule

**Make no election and use the issuer's documented default.**

The holder does nothing. Each distribution follows the default described in its
own source; this does not mean every event pays cash or every event pays stock.
Keep an event blocked if its default, exact allocation, entitlement or fractional
terms are unknown. Do not replace missing terms with an all-cash assumption.

| AIV 2020 | Prorated cash branch; reported $1.02924 cash plus $7.17076 stock value per record share |
| SLG 2021 | Stock election subject to proration; exact holder allocation still unresolved |
| SLG 2022 | Default not established by the saved evidence; keep blocked |
| MAC 2020 | 20% cash / 80% stock for a holder making no election; final conversion terms still unresolved |

AIV values describe conditional reported terms, not verified broker receipts.
Choosing the proposed rule would resolve a modeling preference. It would not
create missing ex-dates, fix fractional payments, approve rounded quantities or
clear the other data gates.

## Alternatives

- **Prefer cash:** make a cash election when available; apply the actual reported
  proration/lottery conditions. AIV 2019 lottery selection cannot be assumed.
- **Prefer stock:** make a stock election when available; apply reported
  proration and require supported holder quantities.

The proposed no-election rule is simple and consistent, without choosing a
different branch after seeing its performance. All cases still require source
and schedule admission before a performance run.
