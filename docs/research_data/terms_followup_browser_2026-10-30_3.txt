L132@P6: 1. Description of the Plan
L133@P6: The following description of the Plan is provided for general information purposes only. Participants should refer to the Plan document for
L134@P6: a more comprehensive description of the Plan’s provisions. The WK Kellogg Co Bakery, Confectionery, Tobacco Workers and Grain
L135@P6: Millers Savings and Investment Plan (the Plan) (formerly the Kellogg Company Bakery, Confectionery, Tobacco Workers and Grain Millers
L136@P6: Savings and Investment Plan) operates as a qualified defined contribution plan with a 401K feature and was established under Section
L137@P6: 401(a) of the Internal Revenue Code. It is subject to the provisions of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (ERISA).
L138@P6: On October 2, 2023, the Spin-Off of WK Kellogg Co from the former parent was completed, resulting in two independent companies, WK
L139@P6: Kellogg Co and Kellanova.
L140@P6: WK Kellogg Co (the Company) assumed the role of Plan Sponsor and, established the WK Kellogg Co Master Savings and Investment
L141@P6: Trust (WKKC Master S&I Trust). Plan assets were moved from the Kellogg Company Master Trust to the WKKC Master S&I Trust on
L142@P6: August 4, 2023. There was no impact to the benefits of Plan participants.
L143@P6: Plan Administration
L144@P6: The Plan is administered by an administration committee appointed by the Company and the president of the Bakery, Confectionery,
L145@P6: Tobacco Workers and Grain Millers International Union, the ERISA Finance Committee and the ERISA Administrative Committee
L146@P6: appointed by the Company.
L147@P6: The ERISA Finance Committee has appointed Fidelity Investments to provide recordkeeping and financial advisory services to the Plan
L148@P6: and participants.
L149@P6: Plan Participation and Contributions
L150@P6: Generally, all the Company hourly employees belonging to the Bakery, Confectionery, Tobacco Workers and Grain Millers Union Local
L151@P6: Nos. 3-G, 50-G, 252-G, 374-G and 401-G are eligible to participate in the Plan after the completion of their service requirement.
L152@P6: Participants may contribute up to 50% of their eligible compensation, as defined by the Plan, as pretax or Roth contributions, up to the
L153@P6: maximum allowed under the Internal Revenue Code ("IRC"). Participants who have attained age 50 before the end of the year are eligible
L154@P6: to make catch-up contributions. Participants may also contribute rollover amounts representing distributions from other qualified plans.
L155@P6: Employee contributions are matched by the Company at 100% of deferrals on the first 3% of eligible compensation and 50% of deferrals
L156@P6: on the next 2% of eligible compensation. Employee contributions are not matched by the Company until the employee has completed one
L157@P6: year of service from their date of hire.
L158@P6: Participant Accounts

L160@P6: options offered by the Plan, including common stock of WK Kellogg Co and Kellanova. Contributions held in Company common stock and
L161@P6: Kellanova common stock can be transferred by a participant at any time to any other investment fund available under the Plan, except for
L162@P6: transfers prohibited under the Company's Insider Trading Policy. No additional assets may be invested in Kellanova shares after October
L163@P6: 2, 2023, and limits apply to how much may be invested in WK Kellogg Co Shares. Each participant’s account is credited with the
L164@P6: participant’s contribution, the Company’s contribution and Plan earnings, and charged with an

L181@P7: Notes Receivable From Participants
L182@P7: Participants may borrow from their fund accounts a minimum of $1,000 up to a maximum equal to the lesser of $50,000 or 50% of their
L183@P7: account balance. Participants may have only one loan outstanding at any time. Loan transactions are treated as transfers between the
L184@P7: Loan Fund and the other funds. Loan terms range from 12 to 60 months, except for principal residence loans, which must be repaid within
L185@P7: 15 years. Interest is paid at a constant rate equal to one percent over the prime rate in the month the loan begins. Principal and interest
L186@P7: are paid ratably through payroll deductions. Loans uncollectible are deemed distributed and recorded as participant withdrawals.
L187@P7: Payment of Benefits
L188@P7: On termination of service, participants may elect to receive payment of their account balances as a lump sum or installment payments, if
L189@P7: the account balance is over $5,000. Account balances equal to $5,000 or less are paid as a lump sum. Participants with investments in
L190@P7: Company or Kellanova common stock may elect to receive that portion of their distribution in shares. Participants may request an inservice withdrawal of all or a portion of certain types of contributions under standard in-service withdrawal rules.

L200@P8: Investment Valuation and Income Recognition
L201@P8: The investments of the Plan are reported at fair value (except for fully benefit responsive investment contracts, which are reported at
L202@P8: contract value). The fair value of a financial instrument is the amount that would be received to sell an asset or paid to transfer a liability in
L203@P8: an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date (the exit price). Contract value is the relevant measure for
L204@P8: the portion of the net assets available for benefits of a defined contribution plan attributable to fully benefit responsive investment
L205@P8: contracts because contract value is the amount participants normally would receive if they were to initiate permitted transactions under the
L206@P8: terms of the Plan. See Note 3 for discussion of fair value measurements.
L207@P8: Purchases and sales of securities are recorded on a trade-date basis. Interest income is recorded on the accrual basis. Dividends are
L208@P8: recorded on the ex-dividend date. Net appreciation includes the Plan's gains and losses on investments bought and sold as well as held
L209@P8: during the year.

L243@P9: 3. Fair Value Measurements
L244@P9: The Plan’s assets are categorized using a framework for measuring fair value. That framework provides a fair value hierarchy that
L245@P9: prioritizes the inputs to valuation techniques used to measure fair value. The hierarchy gives the highest priority to unadjusted quoted
L246@P9: prices in active markets for identical assets or liabilities (Level 1 measurements) and the lowest priority to unobservable inputs (Level 3
L247@P9: measurements). The three levels of the fair value hierarchy are described below:
L248@P9: Level 1 Inputs to the valuation methodology are unadjusted quoted prices in active markets that are accessible at the measurement
L249@P9: date for identical, unrestricted assets or liabilities.
L250@P9: Level 2 Inputs to the valuation methodology include:
L251@P9: • Quoted prices for similar assets or liabilities in active markets;
L252@P9: • Quoted prices for identical or similar assets or liabilities in inactive markets;
L253@P9: • Inputs other than quoted prices that are observable for the asset or liability; and
L254@P9: • Inputs that are derived principally from or corroborated by observable market data by correlation or other means.
L255@P9: If the asset or liability has a specified (contractual) term, the Level 2 input must be observable for substantially the full term of
L256@P9: the asset or liability.
L257@P9: Level 3 Inputs to the valuation methodology are prices or valuations that require inputs that are both significant to the fair value
L258@P9: measurement and unobservable.
L259@P9: The asset or liability’s fair value measurement level within the fair value hierarchy is based on the lowest level of any input that is
L260@P9: significant to the fair value measurement. Valuation techniques used need to maximize the use of observable inputs and minimize the use
L261@P9: of unobservable inputs.
