L2@P0: Annual Ordinary Dividend Will Increase to $3.64 Per Share
L3@P0: NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Dec. 4, 2020-- SL Green Realty Corp. (NYSE: SLG), Manhattan’s largest office landlord, announced today that its
L4@P0: board of directors has increased SL Green’s annual ordinary dividend by 2.8%, to $3.64 per share on its common stock and OP units. The ordinary
L5@P0: dividend will be paid in cash on a monthly basis. The next ordinary dividend of $0.3033 per share is payable on January 15, 2021 to shareholders of
L6@P0: record at the close of business on December 15, 2020 (the “Record Date”).
L7@P0: The Company also announced that, as a result of asset dispositions in 2020, including the previously announced sale of 410 Tenth Avenue, the board
L8@P0: of directors declared a special dividend with a value of $1.6967 per share, which is payable on January 15, 2021 to shareholders of record on the
L9@P0: Record Date. Shareholders will, therefore, receive a total dividend of $2.00 per share, representing the sum of the ordinary dividend and the special
L10@P0: dividend. The special dividend will be paid in the form of SLG common stock, with the number of shares calculated based on the volume weighted
L11@P0: average trading price of SLG’s common stock between January 5-7, 2021. Shareholders can elect to receive the total dividend in the form of all cash
L12@P0: or all stock, subject to proration if either option is oversubscribed.
L13@P0: "All of us at SL Green have worked tirelessly to navigate the unprecedented challenges of the pandemic. As a result of these efforts, we are now in a
L14@P0: position to not only increase our ordinary dividend, but to also issue a special dividend. We will continue to work aggressively to generate value that
L15@P0: directly benefits our shareholders,” said Matt DiLiberto, Chief Financial Officer.
L16@P0: To mitigate the dilutive impact of the stock issued in the special dividend, the board of directors also authorized a reverse stock split, which will be
L17@P0: effective on January 20, 2021. The split ratio for the reverse stock split will be determined promptly after the close of business on January 7, 2021.
L18@P0: Additional information about the special dividend and the reverse stock split will be filed by SL Green on a Current Report on Form 8-K, which will be
L19@P0: available on the Securities and Exchange Commission website at www.sec.gov.
L20@P0: The board of directors also declared the quarterly dividend on the company’s Series I Preferred Stock for the period October 15, 2020 through and
L21@P0: including January 14, 2021, of $0.40625 per share, payable in cash, which is the equivalent of an annualized dividend of $1.625 per share. The
L22@P0: dividend is payable on January 15, 2021 to shareholders of record at the close of business on December 16, 2020.
L23@P0: About SL Green
L24@P0: SL Green Realty Corp., an S&P 500 company and Manhattan's largest office landlord, is a fully integrated real estate investment trust, or REIT, that is
L25@P0: focused primarily on acquiring, managing and maximizing value of Manhattan commercial properties. As of September 30, 2020, SL Green held
L26@P0: interests in 93 buildings totaling 40.6 million square feet. This included ownership interests in 29.2 million square feet of Manhattan buildings and 10.3
L27@P0: million square feet securing debt and preferred equity investments.
